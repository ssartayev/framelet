# Framelet plot rules

Write an original fictional Reddit-style confession for spoken narration. Use the user's premise and requested language. The voice should sound like someone telling a friend what happened, with concrete details and mixed feelings.

## Draft a story

- Start at the moment an ordinary situation turns personal. Show the action, object, or short line of dialogue that makes the narrator pay attention. Give three ways into that same moment, each compatible with the same setup.
- Establish who is involved, what they owe each other, and what the narrator stands to lose. Give the other person a believable reason to hide, deny, or defend what they did.
- Move through a chain of choices. The narrator sees something, acts on it, gets a response, then has to decide what to do. Every beat adds evidence, raises the stakes, or changes how a previous beat reads. Use two or three strong details that matter later.
- Make the reveal answer the question raised in the hook. A clue already in the setup should make sense after the reveal. A confrontation, admission, or concrete action should show how the narrator learned the truth. Leave room in the ending for the consequence.
- Finish on what the narrator actually did or what changed afterward. If the runtime allows an update, show one later action or consequence that adds new information. Avoid a generic moral, a recap, or a promise of a future payoff.
- Keep the point of view, pronouns, relationships, timeline, and physical facts consistent. Avoid arbitrary coincidences, mysterious new characters, implausible technology, and clues that never matter.

## Replace an ending

- Keep every event, relationship, and object in the existing openings and setup. The old ending is an outcome to replace. A detail found only in that ending has not happened in the new version.
- Use a person and a clue already in the setup to reveal a different motive or outcome. Avoid adding an unrelated subplot. Maintain the narrator's perspective and grammatical gender.
- Show the discovery and the narrator's concrete response in a few short lines. End after a visible consequence. Keep the combined story within the original runtime.

Before returning the requested JSON, silently check that every opening leads into the setup, the evidence supports the reveal, and the listener can say what changed at the end.
